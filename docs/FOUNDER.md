# Founder

**Nicholas Smith** — Founder of **824** and creator of 824 Shield.

Nicholas holds two bachelor's degrees from the **Rochester Institute
of Technology (RIT)**:

- **B.S. in Criminal Justice**
- **B.S. in Information Technology**

That pairing is the DNA of this project. The criminal-justice side
brings the defender's mindset — how attacks are investigated, how
evidence (logs, audit trails, decision records) has to hold up, and
why protecting people comes before protecting systems. The IT side
brings a decade-plus of building the systems themselves: applied AI
engineering at 824, engineering roles at Paychex, and a tenure at
the University of Rochester ending as Senior Analyst/Programmer.

824 Shield exists because its founder has stood on both sides of
the log file: he knows what an attack looks like to the people
investigating it, and what a defense looks like to the people who
have to ship it. It is free software under the GNU GPL v3 because
protection this fundamental should belong to everyone who needs
it — hospitals, towns, small businesses, and the nations they add
up to.
